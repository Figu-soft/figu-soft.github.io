Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

DrawFish is a creative game where users can draw fish and share them in a global aquarium visible to players around the world.

1. Information We Collect

DrawFish only collects the information necessary for the app to function.

The data that may be stored includes:

• The fish drawing created by the user
• The name assigned to the fish
• Upvotes and downvotes made on other users’ drawings

The app does not request real names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any personally identifiable information.

2. Use of Information

User-submitted information is used solely to:

• Display drawings in the app’s global aquarium
• Allow other users to view and vote on drawings
• Maintain a rating system for shared fish

Submitted content (drawings and fish names) may be publicly visible within the app.

3. Data Storage

User-submitted drawings are stored on servers so they can be displayed in the shared aquarium.

No personally identifiable or sensitive data is stored.

4. User Interactions

Users can interact with other players’ drawings through:

• Upvotes
• Downvotes
• Reporting inappropriate content

These interactions are used only to improve the experience and help moderate content.

5. Advertising and Monetization

DrawFish displays ads within the app to help keep it free.

Advertising may be provided by third-party platforms such as Google AdMob. These services may automatically collect certain information, such as device identifiers, approximate IP address, or ad interaction data, in order to show relevant ads and measure performance.

DrawFish does not have direct access to or control over the data collected by these advertising providers. The use of such data is governed by the privacy policies of the respective services.

For more information about how Google uses data collected through its advertising services, users can review Google’s privacy policy.

6. User-Generated Content

Drawings and names submitted by users are considered user-generated content. Users should avoid submitting offensive, inappropriate, or rights-infringing material.

Content that violates these guidelines may be removed.

7. User Privacy

DrawFish is designed to be used without creating an account or providing personal information.

The goal is to offer a creative and social experience while protecting user privacy.

8. Content Removal

Users can report inappropriate drawings using the in-app reporting feature. Reported content may be reviewed and removed if necessary.

9. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
